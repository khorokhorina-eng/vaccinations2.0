# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-01-10

This Privacy Policy describes how **CareVax** (the “App”) handles information when you use the App.

## Data We Store

The App stores the following information **locally on your device**:

- Child profile details you enter (such as name, birth date, and selected country)
- Vaccination records and custom vaccines you create
- Onboarding preferences (such as filters and reminder lead time)

This data is stored using Apple’s on-device storage (for example, UserDefaults). The App does **not** require an account to use.

## Purchases

If you purchase a subscription, purchases are processed by Apple using StoreKit. The App does not collect your payment information.

## Data We Collect

The App does not intentionally collect personal information on a server operated by the developer, and does not require you to provide an email address or phone number.

## Data Sharing

The App does not sell your personal data and does not share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for parents/guardians. Do not enter information you are not authorized to provide.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer via the support email listed on the App Store product page.
