## Privacy Policy (CareVax)

This document is provided to satisfy App Store requirements for a publicly accessible privacy policy link inside the app.

### Data we store on your device

- Child profile data (name, date of birth, selected country)
- Vaccination records you create (dates, notes, optional details)
- App settings (filters, UI preferences)
- Subscription status (for premium access)

This data is stored locally on your device. We do not require creating an account.

### Data we collect

CareVax does not collect or transmit your personal data to our servers.

### Third-party services

Purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple (StoreKit / App Store). Apple may process information according to Apple’s privacy practices.

### Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer via the email address listed in the App Store.
